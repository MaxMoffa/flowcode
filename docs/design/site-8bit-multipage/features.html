LEVEL 1-1 · INVENTORY

FEATURES

Everything you carry into the pond, grouped by what it is for.

LEVEL 1-1

TERMINAL

The terminal is the center, everything else revolves around it.

  • Tabbed terminal

    Sessions side by side, draggable between windows, auto-grouped, per-tab zoom, glow on hidden busy tabs.

  • Explorer & Git dock

    Browse, search and manage the active folder. Stage, commit, diff and switch branches.

  • Built-in editor

    Syntax highlighting and a symbol outline, without leaving the app.

  • Your shell

    PowerShell, cmd, bash, zsh, WSL. Chats and agents follow you into WSL too.

LEVEL 1-2

AGENTS

Your AI agents, in a panel and in a chat.

  • Agent panel

    Every session at a glance, resume in one click, launch in its own git worktree.

  • Chat modeNEW

    Talk to any agent in a real chat: images, attachments, mermaid, math, tables.

  • DelegationNEW

    One agent hands a task to another through the Flowcode MCP server.

  • Auto-resume

    Hit a usage limit? The chat resumes by itself when the limit resets.

  • Profiles

    Several accounts per agent, each with its own login and usage gauge.

LEVEL 1-3

REMOTE

Your desktop, from the couch or from another PC.

  • Android app

    Chats, terminals, files, browser and notifications from your phone.

  • PC → PC

    Open another computer's tabs from your desktop, over the same encrypted link.

  • Home widgets

    Three pixel-art Android widgets with your agents' state.

LEVEL 1-4

EXTRAS

The small things that add up.

  • Local dictationNEW

    Whisper on your GPU (Vulkan): 0.4 s to transcribe, nothing leaves the PC.

  • In-app browser

    Native browser panel with tabs, PDF viewer and dev-server previews.

  • Shortcut plugins

    A quick-action bar driven by declarative JSON plugins.

  • Themes

    Light Carta, dark and auto, frosted glass with adjustable opacity.

  • In-app updates

    One click, verified against signed checksums.

  • English & Italian

    Follows your system language; switch any time.
