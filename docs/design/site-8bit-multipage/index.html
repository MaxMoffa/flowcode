WORLD 1 · THE POND

One terminal, everything around it

Flowcode keeps your shell, files and AI agents together, without breaking your flow. Terminal, file manager, editor, a browser to check your usage limits: four windows, and the thread lost every time you switch. Flowcode brings them into one.

  • One folder, one context

    The file explorer and the editor follow the active terminal's folder, even inside WSL. You cd, and the rest of the app comes along.

  • Agents feel at home

    Six agents recognized and shown in a dedicated panel, with how much usage you have left. Talk to them in a real chat too.

  • Light and native

    Built on Tauri, not Electron: it installs in a moment, starts instantly and uses little memory.

  • Extensible without risk

    Plugins are declarative JSON files, not code: install a colleague's plugin without worrying about what it runs.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Tabs, folders and output come back when you reopen the app, and your settings survive updates.

  • Your pocket pond

    An Android companion app follows and answers your agents from anywhere. No account, no VPN.
