## 🧹 No more waiting after /clear

A small patch for the phone. Clearing a chat from your phone used to leave you staring at the waiting frog for almost a minute, then said the computer hadn't answered, even though the fresh chat was ready after a few seconds. Now the new conversation shows up as soon as it exists. Opening a chat on the phone also got a calmer loading effect. The frog is done waiting around. 🐸

### 🛠 Improvements

- **Calmer chat opening on the phone.** While a chat loads, only the glow along the screen edge shows, without the arc travelling round it.

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- **Phone stuck on the waiting frog after /clear.** The phone lost track of the chat while the desktop started its new session, so it waited the full 45 seconds and then showed "The computer didn't answer". It now spots the fresh conversation right away.
