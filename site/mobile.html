Phone

Your desktop,
in your pocket.

Control your desktop’s agents and terminals from your phone. There’s no account to create and no VPN to set up.

The agent asks for permission on both screens. Answer from either one.

01

Pair your phone

Three steps, once.

  1. Install Flowcode-Android-<version>.apk on your phone, allowing unknown sources if Android asks.
  2. On the PC, open Settings → Agents and turn on Allow access from the Flowcode phone app: a QR code appears.
  3. In the app tap Add computer and scan the code.

How it connects

Phone and PC talk through iroh: end-to-end encrypted, peer-to-peer, with a relay only as a last resort. No account. No VPN.

Remote access is off by default and sleeps until you wake it. Any paired device can be revoked at any time from the same settings page.

Security. A paired device can type into your terminals. Only pair phones you trust, and revoke them when you are done.

02

What you can do

Most of the desktop, sized for one hand.

  • Follow and answer agents

    Watch your agents and their usage limits, per profile, and reply to prompts straight from the phone.

  • Open the PC’s terminals

    Open the PC’s terminals and type into them.

  • Browse the PC’s files

    Browse the PC’s files, and send attachments to chats.

  • Browser

    Phone and desktop share browser tabs.

  • Dictation

    Phone audio is transcribed by the PC’s local Whisper. Nothing leaves your machines.

  • Quick actions

    Run quick actions from the shortcuts bar and mirror the desktop’s settings.

  • Background mode

    Opt-in background mode, with notifications when an agent needs you.

  • Biometric lock

    Lock the app behind your fingerprint or face.

  • Home widgets

    Three pixel-art Android widgets with your agents’ state.

03

PC → PC

Open another computer’s tabs from your desktop, over the same encrypted link.
